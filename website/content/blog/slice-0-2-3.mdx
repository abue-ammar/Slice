Slice 0.2.3 is out. It's a small release about the moments a recording goes wrong: you need a break, you fluffed the first line, or you just want to start over. The recording bar now has a button for each of those.

## Pause

Click **Pause** to stop recording without ending it. Get a glass of water, close the notification you didn't want in the take, find the right tab. Click **Resume** when you're ready and Slice carries on. The pause isn't in the video, so the recording jumps straight from where you stopped to where you started again.

## Restart

Fluffed the intro? Click **Restart**. Slice deletes what you've recorded so far, clears anything you've drawn, counts down from three and starts again. No trip to the editor, no take to clean up afterwards.

## Delete

Click **Delete** to end the recording and throw it away. Nothing is saved and no project is made, so the recordings you didn't mean to keep never pile up in your projects.

## A tidier recording bar

The finish button now shows how long you've been recording, and it keeps showing it while you're paused. Pause, Restart and Delete sit right next to it, and the drawing tools open out from the end of the bar like before.

## Install or update

Run this in Terminal:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://slice.rit3zh.dev/install.sh | bash
```

Or [download Slice.dmg](https://github.com/rit3zh/slice/releases/latest) and drag Slice to Applications. If macOS says it can't verify Slice, click **Done**, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. You only need to do this once.

Slice needs macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.
