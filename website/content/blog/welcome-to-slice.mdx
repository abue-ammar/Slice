Hi, and welcome. Slice is a screen recorder and editor for Mac that I've been building because I wanted one that felt like it belonged on a Mac: fast, quiet, and good-looking by default.

You record your screen, camera and microphone. When you stop, the editor opens with the take already sitting on a wallpaper, with padding, rounded corners and a soft shadow, zooms suggested from your clicks and the cursor smoothed out. Often all that's left is trimming the ends and pressing Export.

## What makes it different

There are good screen recorders out there. These are the things I cared about most while making this one:

- **It's a native Mac app.** Slice is written in Swift, with the preview drawn by the same renderer as the export. What you see while editing is the frame you get, at your display's refresh rate.
- **Nothing leaves your Mac.** There's no account and no upload. Transcription and voice enhancement run on your own machine, and they keep working offline.
- **It's free.** No subscription and no watermark. The source is [on GitHub](https://github.com/infinite-toby/screen-recorder).
- **The cursor is data, not pixels.** Slice records the pointer's path, so you can smooth it, make it bigger, change its style or hide it after you've finished recording.
- **Zooms that feel like a camera.** They glide in, hold and ease back out. The Liquid Glass lens magnifies one spot while the rest of the frame stays put.
- **Edit by editing text.** Delete a sentence from the transcript and the matching stretch of video goes with it.
- **Your iPhone, too.** Plug it in and record its screen in a device frame, or drop the take onto a 3D iPhone you can turn and animate.

## An honest note

Slice is still an experiment. I'm building it on my own, it's at version 0.1, and it has bugs. Some I know about, and I'm sure there are plenty I don't yet. An export might fail, a setting might not stick, or something might simply look off on your Mac. If you're recording something important, keep a backup.

I'd rather say that up front than pretend it's finished. It isn't, and the best way for me to make it better is to hear from the people using it.

## How you can help

If you try Slice, here's what helps most:

- **Tell me what broke.** [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/infinite-toby/screen-recorder/issues) with what you did and what happened. A screenshot or a short recording is worth a lot.
- **Tell me what's missing.** The [roadmap](/roadmap) shows what's coming next. If something you need isn't on it, I'd like to know.
- **Share what you make.** Seeing real recordings made in Slice is the best feedback there is, and it helps other people find it.
- **Star the repo or buy me a coffee.** Neither is expected, but both keep this going. You can [support Slice here](https://buymeacoffee.com/rit3zh).

Thanks for being here this early. [Download Slice](https://github.com/infinite-toby/screen-recorder/releases/latest), make something, and let me know how it goes.
